What is Automata Theory?

The mathematical study of abstract machines and the problems they can solve.

Finite Automata

Abstract machines that process strings of symbols over an alphabet. They read one symbol at a time and decide whether to accept or reject the input based on their current state and transitions.

Regular Languages

The class of languages recognised by finite automata. Every regular language can be described by a regular expression and processed by a DFA in linear time.

NFA vs DFA

A Non-deterministic Finite Automaton (NFA) can have multiple transitions for the same symbol. A Deterministic Finite Automaton (DFA) has exactly one. Every NFA can be converted to an equivalent DFA.

Computability

Automata theory forms the foundation for understanding what problems computers can and cannot solve — from compilers and pattern matching to the limits of computation itself.
